## 1. Impact Measurement Framework

The plan commits to tracking **six core KPIs** plus two advanced social-impact measures. This gives investors, grant committees, and the board a defensible evidence trail for the sanctuary's effectiveness.

### 1.1 Core Operating KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Steady-State Target | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Live Release Rate (LRR)** | % of animals leaving alive | **>95%** | "No-Kill" threshold is 90%; a senior-specific sanctuary should exceed it because hospice placements count as live outcomes |
| **Length of Stay (LOS), on-site** | Avg days in sanctuary | **<60 days** for placement-track dogs | Balance between decompression/medical stabilization and throughput; the foster network is the pressure-release valve |
| **LOS, foster network** | Avg days in foster | **60–180 days** for placement-track; **open-ended** for hospice | Fosters extend capacity — target 25–40 active foster homes |
| **Cost of Care per Animal (COCA)** | (Total ops + medical) / (animals served) | **$4,500–$6,500/dog** | Higher than standard shelter COCA (~$500–$1,200) because senior medical is expensive — this is the case for diversion value vs. municipal shelter |
| **Donor Retention Rate (DRR)** | % of prior-year donors who give again | **>65%** Year 3+ | Nonprofit industry median 45%; DRR >65% is the operational stability threshold |
| **Foster Utilization Rate** | Active fosters / committed fosters | **>75%** | Measures whether the foster network is actually absorbing dogs |

### 1.2 Advanced Social-Impact Measures

| KPI | Definition | Why Investors Care |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SROI (Social Return on Investment)** | Monetized social value ÷ total investment | Grant-committee standard; gives donors a dollars-out-for-dollars-in framing |
| **SADs (Suffering-Adjusted Days) Averted** | Difference in stress + pain days between "shelter box" and sanctuary for each dog | Advanced impact-measurement used by effective-altruism-aligned grantmakers; Animal Charity Evaluators reviews use this |

### 1.3 Marketing / Growth KPIs

| KPI | Year-1 Target | Year-3 Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email list size | 2,500 | 15,000 |
| Facebook followers | 10,000 | 75,000 |
| Instagram followers | 5,000 | 30,000 |
| TikTok followers | 2,500 | 25,000 |
| Monthly donors | 100 | 500+ |
| Legacy Hero bequest intentions | 10–20 | 50+ |
| Grant-to-proposal win rate | 30% | 50% |

**Social-media note:** Old Friends SDS took 13 years to build 1.8M Facebook followers. Starting day-one content discipline is the single highest-leverage investment Bailey's World can make in its fundraising future — which is why a Comms/Content Lead is a **Month-3 hire**, not a deferred luxury.

### 2.2 Quality-of-Life (QOL) Assessment

Every senior dog receives a formal QOL assessment at intake and monthly thereafter:
- **HHHHHMM Scale** (Hurt, Hunger, Hydration, Hygiene, Happiness, Mobility, More good days than bad)
- **Lap of Love Quality of Life Scale** — supplemental, used widely in pet-hospice

QOL data feeds the SADs-averted measurement and informs palliative-care decisions.

### 2.3 Annual Impact Report

Published each fiscal-year-end (publicly, on baileysworld.org):
- KPI dashboard (all 6 core + 2 advanced)
- Named dogs served, with photos and outcomes
- Financial summary + 990 link
- Volunteer / foster / donor contributions
- Year-ahead outlook
- This is both donor-stewardship and grant-application supporting evidence

## 3. Staged Raise — Three Gates, Five Checks

Borrows the pitch deck's raise-design logic and builds out the gating criteria. Capital is released in three tranches. Each tranche unlocks only after the prior gate's checks are cleared.

### 3.1 Stage 1 — Pre-Development Pledges ($250K–$500K)

**Use of funds:**
- NC nonprofit formation + IRS 1023 filing + charitable registration
- Fiscal sponsor engagement (180-day bridge)
- Founder compensation (Month 1–6, runway while 1023 pending)
- Land-use attorney engagement (Orange + Chatham UDO review)
- Parcel identification (3–5 candidates)
- Top-2 candidate due diligence:
  - Septic / well feasibility
  - Structural barn assessment
  - Title commitment
  - Zoning interpretation letter
- Comms Lead recruitment begins
- First Grey Muzzle application filed (even before lease)

**Gate-1 checks (must all be green to advance to Stage 2):**
1. ✅ NC 501(c)(3) filed (IRS 1023 acknowledgment letter received)
2. ✅ Fiscal sponsor engaged (bridge for Stage 1 donations)
3. ✅ Parcel identified with written zoning interpretation from county planning
4. ✅ Septic/well feasibility clean (or engineered system cost budgeted)
5. ✅ Barn structural assessment clean (or remediation cost budgeted)

### 3.2 Stage 2 — Land + Diligence Close ($600K–$1M additional)

**Triggered only when all Gate-1 checks are green.**

**Use of funds:**
- Land acquisition (parcel close)
- Architectural design for barn conversion
- SUP / CUP application filing
- Neighborhood meeting + NIMBY outreach
- NCDA&CS Animal Shelter Certificate application
- NCVMB pre-application determination letter (Phase-I facility-permit trigger question)
- Foster Coordinator hire (Month 3–4)
- Vet Tech Manager recruitment begins
- Early foster-network recruitment (goal: 10 committed fosters by end of Stage 2)

**Gate-2 checks (must all be green to advance to Stage 3):**
1. ✅ Land closed under clean title
2. ✅ SUP / CUP approved OR approval path locked in
3. ✅ Conversion plans stamped (architect + structural engineer)
4. ✅ 60–70% of Stage-3 capital committed (silent phase milestone)
5. ✅ First 10 fosters committed

### 3.3 Stage 3 — Conversion + Launch ($1.2M–$3.0M total Phase-I)

**Triggered only when all Gate-2 checks are green.**

**Use of funds:**
- Barn conversion construction (12–24 months)
- Medical equipment + HVAC
- Furnishings + enrichment spaces (catios, meet-and-greet rooms, hospice suites)
- Vet Tech Manager + Animal Care Lead + Ops Coordinator hires
- Comms Lead launch of social-media engine (daily content)
- Volunteer program launch (target: 100 active)
- Certificate of Occupancy → first cohort of seniors on-site
- 12-month post-launch operating reserve (built into raise)

**Gate-3 celebration (marks Phase-I complete):**
1. ✅ Certificate of Occupancy received
2. ✅ First cohort of senior dogs on-site
3. ✅ Social-media flywheel live (daily posts, 10K+ FB followers)
4. ✅ 12-month operating runway funded
5. ✅ Annual Impact Report methodology + dashboard live

### 3.4 Stage-Gate Use-of-Funds Distribution (at $2.1M midpoint)

| Category | % of Raise | $ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Land acquisition | 20–30% | $420K–$630K |
| Barn conversion + medical fit-out | 45–55% | $945K–$1,155K |
| Site work (septic, well, electrical, stormwater) | 10–20% | $210K–$420K |
| Utilities + telecom + IT | 3–5% | $60K–$105K |
| Launch reserve (12-mo operating) | 10–15% | $210K–$315K |

## 5. Phased Growth Beyond Phase I

### Phase II — Meet-and-Greet Expansion (Year 2–3)
- Builds public-facing catios, meet-and-greet rooms, shaded seating
- Converts sanctuary from operating facility to community resource center
- Drives adoption throughput + donor engagement + volunteer recruitment
- **Capital requirement:** $500K–$1M incremental

### Phase III — On-Site Veterinary Clinic (Year 3–4)
- Full NCVMB Facility Permit buildout
- Employed DVM + expanded RVT team
- Low-cost public clinic for senior pets in Triangle (community mission)
- Revenue-generating for Bailey's World-cared dogs + adopter clinic access
- **Capital requirement:** $500K–$1.5M incremental
- **2025 NCVMB permit ($150/yr) + facility minimum standards (21 NCAC 66 .0207) + supervising DVM 40+ hr/mo**

### Phase IV — Regional Replication (Year 5+)
- Bailey's World as "proof of concept" for NC's vast agricultural landscape
- Partnership model with other NC regions (Charlotte metro, Asheville, Wilmington)
- National affiliations — Best Friends Network senior-dog flagship status

## 6. Exit / Sustainability Strategy

Bailey's World is a nonprofit — "exit" is a misnomer. The question is **long-term sustainability**. Three structural pillars:

### 6.1 Endowment

- Target: **$3M endowment** by Year 7
- Funded primarily by planned giving (Legacy Hero + Bailey's Retirement Fund programs)
- Industry rule-of-thumb: endowment ÷ operating budget ≥ 3 = operational durability
- Implication for Bailey's World at $800K–$1.2M steady-state budget: **$3M endowment supports ~3–4 yr operating buffer**

### 6.2 Diversified Revenue Mix (Year 5 target)

| Revenue Source | Year-5 Target % |
| --- | --- |
| Individual donations | 50% |
| Planned giving / bequests realized | 10% |
| Grants (Grey Muzzle, Petco Love, Maddie's, Best Friends, etc.) | 15% |
| Boarding revenue (social enterprise LLC) | 10% |
| Clinic revenue (Phase III) | 5% |
| Events | 5% |
| Corporate sponsorships / in-kind | 5% |

No single source >50%. No single donor >10% of annual revenue.

### 6.3 Succession Planning

- Founder + ED role: 10-year commitment from Wally
- Identified successor candidates on Board by Year 5
- Documented operational playbooks (the MVP is the Year-1 Operations Manual)
- Bailey's World Inc. must outlive its founder — that's the test

## 7. What Success Looks Like

**Year 1 (End of Stage 3):**
- Bailey's World operational. 20–25 senior dogs on-site. 25–40 foster homes active. Annual budget ~$650K. 10K Facebook followers. First cohort of Legacy Heroes named.

**Year 3:**
- 150 dogs/year steady-state. 500 monthly donors. NC State CVM rotation running. First bequest realized. Phase II meet-and-greet operational. 50K Facebook followers.

**Year 5:**
- 200+ dogs/year. Phase III clinic open. $3M endowment pipeline. NC's flagship senior-dog sanctuary. Replicable model being studied by other NC regions.

**Year 10:**
- Bailey's World is what SPCA Wake is today for the Triangle: the reference animal-welfare institution of the region. Every senior dog in the Triangle knows a Bailey's World adopter or foster. The "retirement phase" is no longer an aspiration — it's what Triangle senior dogs get.
