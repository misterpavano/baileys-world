# Appendix E — Capital Stack & Funding Strategy
## Bailey's World / Triangle Senior Dog Barn Sanctuary

*Phase-one capitalization: **$1.25M–$3.05M**. Target midpoint: $2.15M. Supporting research: `plan/research/03_capital_stack.md`.*

---

## 1. Capital Stack Thesis

Bailey's World is **structured mission capital, not venture equity.** The backer logic is charitable impact-first, with optional recoverable or real-estate-aligned instruments where the donor-investor wants their capital to recycle. Investors are not buying a return; they are buying **the retirement phase Bailey got** — at scale, visibly, in their community.

Three parallel funding streams are intentional by design:

1. **Founding gifts + naming rights** — the emotional core of the campaign; the donors who want their name on Bailey's World for life
2. **DAFs + recoverable grants** — the silent fuel; Triangle Community Foundation and national DAFs flow 25–40% of a typical major-gift campaign
3. **Program-related investments + low-cost real-estate debt** — the leverage tool; Durham-local CDFI Self-Help is the first call for up to 18% of the raise

Two supporting streams:

4. **Sector capital grants** — Grey Muzzle, Petco Love, PetSmart Charities, ASPCA, Best Friends, Banfield (operating/program-sized, bridges construction-phase cash flow)
5. **Small-donor + events** — the community-credibility layer; necessary for board legitimacy and for DAF-holder due diligence

## 5. Bucket #3 — PRI / Low-Cost Real-Estate Debt ($380K / 18%)

### 5.1 Self-Help Credit Union (Durham, NC) — Local Priority

- **CDFI headquartered in Durham** — Bailey's World is in Self-Help's home market
- Nonprofit lending criteria: 2+ years operating history, 2–3 years tax returns, collateral (typically real estate), cash investment required
- NC loan sizes historically capped <$500K unless branch market (expanding — verify at application)
- SBA 504/7(a) programs available via Self-Help

**First-call recommendation.** Self-Help's Durham HQ proximity + mission alignment + CDFI flexibility + NC-chartered stance makes it the single highest-probability institutional capital partner for Bailey's World.

### 5.2 Alternative / Complementary Sources

| Source | Rate (est.) | Term | Best Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recoverable Grants (DAF/foundation) | 0% | ≤10 yr | Bridge-to-pledge |
| Calvert Impact Capital Community Note (indirect) | 0.4–2.5% | 1–10 yr | Via CDFI intermediary |
| RSF Social Finance | ~4–6% | 3–10 yr | Must align with regeneration themes |
| Nonprofit Finance Fund | 4.5–7% | 5–25 yr | Flexible LTV |
| **Self-Help Credit Union** | **5.5–7.5%** | **7–25 yr** | **Primary real-estate loan** |
| Kresge Community Finance (via CDFI) | 2–5% | 5–15 yr | Indirect channel |

### 5.3 PRI Structure Framework

A Bailey's World real-estate acquisition PRI would typically sit as:
- **40–60% philanthropic equity (gifts)** → from Buckets #1 + #2
- **30–50% PRI or CDFI loan** → Self-Help primary, RSF or NFF secondary
- **10–20% bridge pledges** → recoverable grants

Debt service discipline: keep total debt <20% of annual operating budget post-construction. For a $500K–$700K steady-state operating budget, that caps allowable debt service at $100K–$140K/yr.

## 8. Entity Structure Recommendation

**Primary entity:** 501(c)(3) nonprofit — "Bailey's World Inc." or similar

**Formation costs (NC):**
- NC Articles of Incorporation: $60
- IRS Form 1023 (full): $600
- NC Charitable Solicitation License: $0–$200 (sliding scale)
- Legal (attorney-drafted bylaws, conflict of interest policy, document retention policy): $2K–$5K
- **Total startup cost: ~$2,700–$5,900**

**Timeline:**
- NC incorporation: 2–4 weeks
- IRS 501(c)(3) determination (Form 1023): ~180 days
- Form 1023-EZ faster (2–4 weeks) but sanctuary exceeds $50K gross eligibility

**Bridge strategy:** Engage a fiscal sponsor (e.g., NC Center for Nonprofits partner org) during the 180-day IRS determination period. Sponsor fee typically 5–10% of donations.

**Social-enterprise arm (when to add):**
- If boarding revenue projected **>$100K/year**, form a wholly-owned taxable LLC subsidiary
- Nonprofit owns 100% of LLC; LLC operates public boarding; profits distributed as dividend to nonprofit
- Cleaner IRS treatment than running boarding as UBTI inside 501(c)(3)
- **L3C not available in NC** (form eliminated 2014)
- Defer LLC formation until boarding revenue is 12 months from launch
