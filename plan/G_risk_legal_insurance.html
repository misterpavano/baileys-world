3. Legal Entity Structure

3.1 Primary: 501(c)(3) Public Charity

  • Legal name: Bailey’s World Inc. (or similar — trademark search + NC Secretary of State name availability check required)
  • State: NC nonprofit corporation
  • Federal: 501(c)(3) public charity (Form 1023 full, not EZ — sanctuary exceeds $50K eligibility cap)
  • Board: Minimum 3 unrelated directors; target 5–7 Year 1

3.2 Governance Documents

Required for IRS determination + donor due diligence: - Articles of Incorporation - Bylaws - Conflict of Interest Policy (IRS Form 1023 Appendix A standard) - Document Retention & Destruction Policy - Whistleblower Policy - Gift Acceptance Policy (including cryptocurrency, real estate, planned giving) - Executive Compensation Policy (with board approval + comparability data) - Volunteer Agreement + Waiver - Foster Home Agreement

Drafting cost: $2K–$5K via NC nonprofit legal counsel (NC Pro Bono Resource Center matches possible); Foundation Group / Harbor Compliance package services ~$3K–$5K.

3.3 Phase-III Addition: Taxable LLC Subsidiary (If Boarding Revenue >$100K/Yr)

  • 100% nonprofit-owned LLC
  • LLC operates public boarding service; profits distributed as dividend to nonprofit
  • Cleaner IRS treatment than running boarding as UBTI inside 501(c)(3)
  • L3C not available in NC (eliminated 2014). If considered, form in VT/MI/IL and foreign-qualify — but standard LLC with social-enterprise operating agreement is simpler.

3.4 Fiscal Sponsorship (Pre-Formation Bridge)

  • Purpose: receive tax-deductible donations during ~180-day IRS determination window
  • Candidates: NC Center for Nonprofits member orgs; local animal-welfare fiscal sponsors
  • Typical fee: 5–10% of donations
  • Duration: ~6–8 months max

3.5 NC Charitable Solicitation License

  • Required to solicit donations in NC
  • Fee: $0–$200 on a sliding scale by gross contributions
  • Annual renewal

4. Intellectual Property

4.1 Trademarks to Secure

  • “Bailey’s World” — wordmark + logo (Class 36 Charitable fundraising; Class 43 Animal sheltering and boarding; Class 44 Veterinary services)
  • “Triangle Senior Dog Barn Sanctuary” — descriptive subtitle (lower trademark strength)
  • “Bailey’s Best Friends” — monthly-donor program
  • “Legacy Hero” / “Legacy Care” — if adopting Forever Loved’s program structure, avoid confusing similarity; consider alternative names

Cost: $250–$350 USPTO filing per class + $500–$1,500 legal per mark for search + filing. Budget $2K–$4K for the trademark portfolio Year 1.

4.2 Domain & Social Handles

  • Secure: baileysworld.org (primary), baileysworldnc.org, .com variants
  • Social handles: @baileysworldnc across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky
  • Email infrastructure: Google Workspace Nonprofit (free tier) OR Microsoft 365 Nonprofit

4.3 Content & Photo Rights

  • Work-for-hire agreements with all contract photographers/videographers
  • Model release for adopter and volunteer on-camera appearances
  • Foster family publication authorization (can opt out of social posts)

5.1 Specialist Carriers (Ranked for Bailey’s World Fit)

  1. AnimalKeepers (McNeil & Co.) — shelter-specific program, broadest animal-welfare experience
  2. Higginbotham Animal Welfare — broker representing multiple markets
  3. AWOIP (Animal Welfare Organization Insurance Program) — dedicated program for nonprofits
  4. The Hartford Animal Rescue — mass-market, competitive pricing
  5. Vetinsure — veterinary-nonprofit specialist (Phase III relevance)

5.2 Volunteer Coverage

Most shelter-specific policies include volunteer extension. Verify “care, custody, control” exclusions — these can silently exclude foster-home incidents. Foster coverage must be explicit, not assumed.

5.3 Adopter Release

Every adoption contract includes: - Assumption of risk (senior dogs have known medical conditions) - Right of Bailey’s World to reclaim for neglect or abuse - Disclosure of all known medical conditions and behaviors - Post-adoption support commitment (medical stipend tier, if applicable)
