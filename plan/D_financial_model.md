# Appendix D — Financial Model
## Bailey's World / Triangle Senior Dog Barn Sanctuary

*Five-year pro forma, CapEx build, OpEx schedule, unit economics, and three scenarios. Integrates capital stack (Appendix E), site strategy (Appendix C), operating model (Appendix B), staffing (Appendix F). Flagged as directional — final numbers lock at term-sheet stage.*

## 1. Financial Strategy Thesis

Bailey's World is a **non-profit** — financial success means **durable mission delivery at scale**, not margin. The design principles:

1. **Phase-I capitalization sized to reach operational viability** ($1.2M–$3.0M) — not to reach every aspirational phase
2. **Every CapEx dollar tied to a gated milestone** (see Appendix H §3)
3. **6–12 months of operating reserve** baked into the Phase-I raise, not raised later
4. **Social-enterprise revenue (boarding, clinic) phased in only after core sanctuary stabilizes** — do not chase earned revenue while still building infrastructure
5. **Donor revenue targets <50% from any single source; <10% from any single donor** at steady state

## 5. Unit Economics

### 5.1 Cost of Care per Animal (COCA)

**Steady-state senior sanctuary COCA:** $1,800–$6,500/dog depending on track:

| Track | Avg Annual COCA | Drivers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green (healthy senior, adoption track) | $1,800–$2,800 | Dental, exam, routine meds, basic intake workup |
| Yellow (diagnostic / chronic mgmt) | $3,000–$4,500 | Bloodwork, imaging, specialist referrals, arthritis meds |
| Red (hospice / palliative) | $4,500–$6,500 | Complex comorbidity mgmt, hospice vet time, end-of-life care |

**Weighted avg at Bailey's World mix (60% green, 25% yellow, 15% red):** ~$2,900/dog

Industry benchmark: Dakin Humane blended (all species) $950/yr. Senior-specific COCA runs 2–4× due to medical intensity. Peer: Austin Pets Alive! reports ~$2,400 per dog annually for standard-care dogs.

### 5.2 Revenue per Dog

| Revenue Type | Per-Dog Contribution |
| --- | --- |
| Adoption fee (green track) | $150–$350 |
| Sponsor-a-Senior matched (avg) | $50–$150/mo × LOS |
| Planned-giving backstop (amortized) | Highly variable; not directly allocated |
| **Weighted avg revenue per placement** | ~$250–$500 |

**COCA - revenue per dog = gap of ~$2,400–$2,650/dog**, filled by donations, DAFs, and grants. This is the diversion-value case to donors: you are personally buying the retirement phase for a specific senior dog.

### 5.3 Adoption Fee Sensitivity

Raising avg adoption fee from $250 → $400 adds ~$30K/yr at 200 dogs/yr. Material but not transformative. Sanctuary model is donor-funded, not adoption-fee-funded — consistent with peer sanctuaries (OFSDS charges $0; Muttville $250 often waived).

## 6. Social-Enterprise Revenue Detail

### 6.1 Public Boarding (Phase II, Year 3)

**Pricing:**
- Standard senior boarding: $65/night
- Medicated surcharge: +$15/night (for dogs on 3+ meds or injectables)

**Market validation:**
- Raleigh boarding median $45/night (range $26–$59)
- Vet-adjacent boarding with meds: $50–$80/night
- Bailey's World premium positioning: "senior-safe medicated boarding" — underserved niche

**Capacity + utilization:**
- 6–10 boarding suites (separate from sanctuary suites)
- 70% utilization target → 8 suites × 365 × 0.70 × avg $70/night = **~$143K/yr** at full ramp

### 6.2 On-Site Vet Clinic (Phase III, Year 4)

**Revenue drivers:**
- Public dental cleanings (the #1 senior service) — $300–$700 per dog
- Senior wellness exams + bloodwork — $150–$250
- Palliative/hospice specialty — limited competition in Triangle; premium pricing defensible
- Means-tested pricing generates subsidy flow from market-rate clients

**Ramp:** Year 3 bridge ($0), Year 4 $50K–$175K, Year 5 $125K–$325K. Clinic breakeven typically Year 5+.

## 7. Key Ratios & Covenants

### 7.1 Debt Service Coverage

**Policy: Total debt service ≤ 18% of annual operating budget** at steady state.

| Year | Operating Budget | Max Debt Service | Implied Debt @ 6% / 15-yr |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Y1 | $875K | $157K | $1.85M |
| Y3 | $1.4M | $252K | $2.97M |
| Y5 | $2.2M | $396K | $4.67M |

Phase-I debt target (Self-Help / PRI stack): $380K at $2.1M midpoint = 18% of raise; **modest debt service ~$38K/yr** — well within policy.

### 7.2 Fundraising Ratio

- **Industry target:** Fundraising cost / total fundraising revenue ≤ 25%
- Bailey's World Year 1 (front-loaded capital campaign): up to 35% acceptable
- Year 3+: target ≤ 20%

### 7.3 Board-Policy Financial Floors

1. **Operating reserve: ≥12 months** (board review quarterly)
2. **Debt service: ≤18% of operating budget**
3. **No single donor: ≤25% of a capital campaign; ≤10% of annual operating revenue** at steady state
4. **Program services ratio: ≥75%** (program expense / total expense) — Year 2+
5. **Endowment target: $3M by Year 7** (per Appendix H)
